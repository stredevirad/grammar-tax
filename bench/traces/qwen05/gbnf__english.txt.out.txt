The printing press, a revolutionary invention, revolutionized the world of book production in the 15th and 16th centuries. Initially developed in India, the printing press spread throughout Europe and further around the world during the 15th and 16th centuries. The printing press made books more affordable, accessible, and widespread. This technological advancement had a profound impact on intellectual property rights, literature, and cultural exchange. The printing press also played a significant role in the spread of Protestantism in Europe and its influence on other world religions.
